Manuscript & Martyr

A personal tool for producing and publishing short videos about early Christian history.

What it is

Manuscript & Martyr is a piece of software I built and run on my own computer. It researches a topic, writes a short script, generates narration and visuals, assembles the video, and publishes it on a schedule to my own social media accounts — @manuscriptmartyr on TikTok, and the channel of the same name on YouTube.

It exists because making these videos by hand takes hours each and I would rather spend that time on the history than on the editing.

What it is not

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, no hosted version, and no way for anyone else to use it or have it act on their account. It runs on one computer, for one person, and publishes to that person's own accounts.

How it works

  • Researches a subject and drafts a short script.
  • Generates the narration with a text-to-speech voice.
  • Generates or sources the visuals — either AI-generated images, or openly licensed photographs and footage from stock libraries.
  • Assembles the finished vertical video with ffmpeg.
  • Uploads it to my own accounts at a time I have chosen.

Every video is reviewed by me before it is scheduled, and the schedule is set far enough ahead that I can pull anything back before it goes out.

AI-generated content

The narration and most of the imagery are machine-generated, and uploads are marked as containing AI-generated or synthetic media where the platform provides a way to declare it. The history itself is researched rather than invented, but the voice you hear and most of what you see on screen are generated.
