Manuscript & Martyr · Last updated 8 August 2026

These terms cover Manuscript & Martyr, a personal content-production tool operated by Ockert Nel ("the operator"). It runs on the operator's own computer and publishes videos to the operator's own social media accounts.

What it does

It researches a subject, writes a short script, generates narration and visuals, sources openly licensed stock media, assembles the finished video, and uploads it on a schedule the operator sets.

Who may use it

Only the operator. It is not offered to the public. There is no sign-up, no user accounts, no hosted instance, and no means by which anyone else can access it or have it act on their behalf. It is not a product, a service or a platform.

There are therefore no user obligations, no acceptable-use rules for third parties and no support commitments, because there is nobody else involved.

Accounts and authorisation

It acts only on accounts the operator has personally connected through each platform's standard OAuth flow, and only within the permissions granted during that flow. It never acts on any account belonging to anyone else.

The operator may disconnect any account from within the tool at any time, and may also revoke access from the platform's own settings. Either takes effect immediately.

Content

All published content is produced by or for the operator, who is solely responsible for it.

  • Generated content — scripts, narration and imagery are produced by AI models at the operator's direction. Uploads are marked as containing AI-generated or synthetic media where the platform provides a way to declare it.
  • Stock media — footage and photographs come from providers whose licences permit the use, and attribution is recorded against every asset.
  • Nothing is republished from another creator without rights. A reuse-risk classification is recorded against every media asset so that rights decisions are visible at the point footage is chosen.

Platform rules

The tool is operated in accordance with the terms of service, community guidelines and developer policies of every platform it connects to, including TikTok and YouTube. Where a platform restricts what an unaudited application may do, that restriction is respected rather than worked around.

Automation

Videos are published on a schedule. The tool does not artificially inflate engagement, does not create or operate multiple accounts to manipulate distribution, does not post spam or repetitive low-value content, and does not interact with other users' content automatically in any way. Its automation is limited to producing the operator's own videos and uploading them at chosen times.
